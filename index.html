Ropafadzo Liberty Mzondo

Zimbabwean National Sprinter & High School Record Holder

Athlete Biography

Ropafadzo Liberty Mzondo is a highly promising sprint athlete from Zimbabwe who represents Goldridge College and the national junior athletics teams. Known for his versatility across short sprints, he has established himself as one of the country's most formidable junior talents in the 200m and 400m disciplines.

Key Career Milestones

August 2026
World Athletics Under-20 Championships

Qualified to represent Zimbabwe at the World Athletics Under-20 Championships in the United States, managing to achieve qualification while still eligibility-wise an Under-18 competitor.

March 2026
NASH Athletics Nationals & Records

Secured individual victories in the 200m and 400m events at the National High School Athletics Competitions in Harare. Set historic regional school standards, running a 21.00s 200m and breaking 400m records dating back to 1974.

March 2026
Midlands Community Trailblazer Award

Awarded special recognition for global sporting impact and representing Goldridge Schools at the Midlands Community Trailblazers Awards.

July 2025
CAA African U18 Championships (Abeokuta, Nigeria)

Represented Team Zimbabwe internationally, successfully reaching the finals of both the Under-18 Men's 200m and 400m sprints on the continental stage.
